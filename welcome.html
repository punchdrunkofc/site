Who—or what—is PunchDrunk?

Let’s clarify one thing: I won’t be describing individual tracks here, as you can find all that information on my YouTube channel. Instead, I’ll talk about the project itself.

I launched this project on August 2, 2023—not with a specific goal in mind, but simply for myself, as an online archive to preserve my musical work forever. The initial spark came from a track that left a lasting mark on my musical taste: Epsyle - Couture. Once the first 1,000 people subscribed, I began to evolve the project—moving beyond the scope of a mere hobbyist musician and developing it into a full-fledged creative endeavor. This site gathers information on all the characters created under the PunchDrunk banner, including those developed by other contributors who have since joined the project.

So, what exactly is PunchDrunk? Honestly, even we don’t know for sure. Everyone defines PunchDrunk in their own way: for some, it’s a heavy, hardcore act that conjures an atmosphere of chaos; for others, it’s a dance-oriented house project built entirely around high-energy movement. As for my own take... PunchDrunk is my soul, poured into every single piece of work. It’s not music for everyone, yet it resonates with many.

Think about what PunchDrunk means to you.
